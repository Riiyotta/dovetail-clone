Turn raw data into rich context

Dovetail’s multimodal engine automatically turns millions of pieces of raw feedback, from text and documents to audio and video, into structured customer context ready for your next prompt.

Book a demoStart free trial

Multimodal data processing

Indexation

Every piece of feedback is indexed the moment it lands, ready for keyword and semantic search across your workspace.

Classification

Feedback is sorted into themes, topics, and sentiment automatically, using the structure your team defines.

Synthesis

Patterns across thousands of conversations come together into clear themes and summaries you can act on.

Embedding

Content is turned into vector embeddings, so AI can find related feedback by meaning, not just keywords.

Enrichment

Feedback is enriched with customer and account data, so every insight carries the context of who said it.

Redaction

Dovetail automatically finds personal and confidential information based on your own instructions, then blurs or deletes it.

Transcription

Every call, interview, and video is transcribed automatically, with each line linked back to the moment it was said.

Translation

Dovetail automatically translates transcripts into another language using the latest LLM translation models.

Diarization

Speakers are detected and separated in every recording, so you always know who said what.

Optical Character Recognition

Text in images, screenshots, and scanned documents is extracted and made searchable with the rest of your feedback.
